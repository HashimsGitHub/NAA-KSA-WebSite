Community Charter

How we participate together.

VCNITY is intended to be a safe, culturally aware and inclusive digital home for community participation. This charter sets out the shared principles that help members, contributors and administrators create that environment together.

Individual community organisations may add their own specific rules or moderation practices, but these principles provide a common foundation.

Shared principles

A community space should work for the people in it.

01

Respect people

Communicate respectfully. Disagreement is allowed; harassment, intimidation, personal attacks and deliberately harmful behaviour are not.

02

Make participation safer

Avoid content or behaviour that creates unnecessary risk for other members. Raise concerns through the community's moderation or administration process.

03

Include different ways of participating

Be considerate of disability, neurodivergence, language, culture, digital confidence and different communication needs.

04

Respect privacy

Do not share another person's private or personal information outside the community without appropriate permission.

05

Respect community knowledge

Give appropriate recognition to the people and communities who create ideas, resources, stories and project outcomes. Do not assume community material is free to reuse elsewhere.

06

Contribute constructively

Use VCNITY to help coordinate activities, share useful knowledge, support projects and strengthen the community rather than disrupt it.

Community ownership

Community creates. Community owns.

VCNITY is built around the principle that community organisations should retain authority over their own information and intellectual property.

Members should treat community-created materials with care and follow the relevant community's expectations about attribution, sharing and reuse.

Moderation and administration

Clear boundaries support healthy participation.

Community administrators and authorised contributors may moderate content and participation where necessary to support safety, accessibility, respectful communication and the purpose of the community space.

Depending on the situation, moderation may include contacting a member, editing or removing inappropriate content, limiting access to specific features, or suspending access to the community hub.

Moderation should be proportionate, understandable and consistent with the needs and governance practices of the community organisation.

Privacy and member information

Share only what is appropriate to share.

Member information should be used for legitimate community purposes. Members should avoid copying, publishing or redistributing personal information from the Member Directory or other protected areas without an appropriate reason and permission.

Where profile visibility controls are available, members and administrators should use them to support appropriate sharing of contact information.

Inclusive participation

Make it easier for others to take part.

When posting resources, planning activities or communicating with other members, consider whether the information is understandable and accessible to people with different abilities, language backgrounds and levels of digital confidence.

VCNITY is intended to evolve with the communities that use it. Community feedback about accessibility, safety and participation should inform how the hub improves over time.

Living charter

A foundation that can evolve with the community.

This charter is a practical community guideline for the VCNITY portal. It can be refined as community organisations test the platform, identify new needs and develop their own governance practices.
