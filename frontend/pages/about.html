Get to know me

I'm Dr Ehud Joseph, founder of VCNITY.

LinkedIn

My PhD at QUT explored how queer communities negotiate identity through creative co-design, and my organisation COLLAB has delivered community programs across Brisbane with Brisbane Festival, Melt Festival, and Aboriginal community centres.

That work taught me that lived experience is expertise, and that expertise deserves to impact communities' lives.

VCNITY is the infrastructure I wish had existed throughout it all.

Why VCNITY

Community creates. Community owns.

VCNITY is being developed as a safer digital home where communities can connect, organise, share knowledge and collaborate without giving up the value of their own information, experience and intellectual property.

The platform is shaped around inclusive participation, community agency, clear governance and practical digital tools that help people work together.

A digital home for communities to connect, collaborate and share.
